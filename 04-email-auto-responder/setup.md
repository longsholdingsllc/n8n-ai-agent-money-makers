# Intelligent Email Auto-Responder – Setup Guide

## What it does
Watches a Gmail or Outlook inbox, uses context (previous threads + CRM if available) to draft high-quality replies, and either sends automatically for simple cases or queues for human approval.

## Value to client
Cuts email response time dramatically while maintaining quality and personalization.

## Suggested pricing
- One-time: $697–$1,497
- Digital product: $49

## Notes
Add memory or previous conversation history for better results. Always keep a human approval step for high-stakes emails.
